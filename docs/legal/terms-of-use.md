## 4. User Responsibilities & Cryptographic Reality

* **Key Custody:** Ember is an end-to-end encrypted relay. The decryption key is generated on your local device and resides entirely in the URL link fragment. We do not have access to your keys and cannot recover drops if you lose your link or passphrase.
* **Ephemeral Storage Notice:** All drops automatically expire and are permanently deleted within twelve (12) hours or upon first view (if burn-after-view is selected). The Service is a transient transfer mechanism and **not** a long-term backup or storage platform.
* **Recipient Retention:** While Ember enforces burn-after-view and screenshot blocking (`FLAG_SECURE`) on standard clients, recipients retain physical control over their hardware and may save decrypted files or photograph screens with external devices.
